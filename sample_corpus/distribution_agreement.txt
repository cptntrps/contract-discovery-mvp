EXHIBIT 10.2
DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

This Distribution Agreement is made between Northwind Products Corp. and Contoso Channels Inc.
Northwind appoints Contoso as a non-exclusive distributor for covered products in California, Oregon, and Washington.
Contoso may resell the products only through approved retail channels.
The initial term is two years. Either party may terminate for uncured material breach after thirty days notice.
Pricing, minimum purchase commitments, and quarterly sales reports are described in Schedule A.
The agreement is governed by Delaware law.
