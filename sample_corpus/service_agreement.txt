EXHIBIT 10.3
MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement is entered into by Example Health Inc. and Meridian Analytics LLC.
Meridian will provide implementation, reporting, and support services described in statements of work.
Fees are payable monthly within thirty days after invoice.
Each party will protect confidential information and comply with applicable data security requirements.
The agreement starts on March 15, 2025 and renews annually unless either party gives notice of non-renewal.
This agreement is governed by the laws of Texas.
