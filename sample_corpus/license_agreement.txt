EXHIBIT 10.1
TECHNOLOGY LICENSE AGREEMENT

This Technology License Agreement is entered into by Alpha Software Inc. and Beta Retail LLC.
Alpha grants Beta a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the licensed software in the United States.
The agreement begins on January 1, 2025 and continues for three years unless terminated earlier.
Beta shall pay Alpha a royalty equal to five percent of net subscription revenue.
The parties agree that confidential information must be protected for five years after disclosure.
This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
